Blueprint 01

Automated lead operations

A connected enquiry journey that reduces manual triage while keeping an owner responsible for the opportunity.

  • Capture structured information from a high-converting form.
  • Classify the service, urgency and fit against agreed rules.
  • Create or update a CRM record and assign the right person.
  • Draft an acknowledgement using approved language.
  • Escalate incomplete or unusual enquiries for human review.

Blueprint 02

Content Approval & Publishing Workflow

A repeatable system for producing consistent social posts without losing brand control or publishing unapproved work.

  • Set content themes, platforms, formats and monthly priorities.
  • Prepare copy and visuals in a visible production queue.
  • Request consolidated comments from authorised reviewers.
  • Record approval before content enters the scheduling queue.
  • Use performance learning to shape the next cycle.

Blueprint 03

Customer request portal

A focused web application that replaces scattered emails with a clear status, accountable ownership and relevant notifications.

  • Secure sign-in and role-based access.
  • Structured request form with supporting files.
  • Internal workflow stages, notes and assignment.
  • Customer-visible progress and approved documents.
  • Automated reminders and exception alerts.
