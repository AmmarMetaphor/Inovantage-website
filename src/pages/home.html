Review-first publishing

Nothing should publish by accident.

The included content system separates drafts from reviewed work. Editors can prepare an article, move it into review, check a private preview and publish only after approval.

The same principle can be used for social media: create the calendar, prepare copy and visuals, request comments, record approval, then schedule.
