Frequently asked questions

Website project basics.

Can you use our existing logo and brand?

Yes. We can work with an established design system or build a practical web style around the assets you already have. High-quality source files and any brand rules are helpful.

Who writes the website content?

We can structure and draft content based on interviews and source material, collaborate with your writer, or work from copy you provide. Content responsibilities are agreed at the start so the project keeps moving.

Can our team update the site?

Yes. The included content manager allows authorised users to update business details and publish insight articles without touching code. Larger layout changes can be made through the code repository.

Will the website work on mobile?

Yes. Layouts and interactions are designed and tested across common screen sizes, with touch targets, navigation and form controls adapted for smaller devices.

Is your current website helping buyers choose your business?

Tell us where it is underperforming, what your customers need to understand and which actions matter commercially.
