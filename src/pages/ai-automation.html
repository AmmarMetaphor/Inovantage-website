Frequently asked questions

Before starting an automation project.

Do we need to replace our existing software?

Often, no. Many useful automations connect tools you already use. We first check the available APIs, permissions and data quality before recommending a change.

Can an AI system publish or contact customers automatically?

It can, but that is not always wise. For higher-risk messages or public content, we normally recommend a review and approval step until the workflow has demonstrated reliable performance.

How do we choose the first process?

Look for work that happens often, follows repeatable rules, causes delays and has a measurable outcome. Avoid beginning with a rare process full of exceptions.

What happens when the automation fails?

The workflow should log the failure, notify an owner and preserve enough context for a person to complete or retry the task. Failure handling is part of the design, not an afterthought.

Which repeated task would you most like to remove?

We can assess the workflow and recommend a safe, practical first automation.
