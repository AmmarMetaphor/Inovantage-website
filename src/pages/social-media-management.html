Approval workflow

Review posts before publication.

Every post follows a visible path, so stakeholders know what needs attention and nothing is scheduled without the required approval.

  1. Draft: copy and creative are prepared against the agreed calendar.
  2. In review: your team receives a review window and leaves comments in one place.
  3. Ready: requested changes are complete and an authorised reviewer approves the post.
  4. Scheduled: the approved version is placed in the publishing queue.
  5. Reported: results and useful learning feed into the next content cycle.

Frequently asked questions

How the service works.

Can we approve every post?

Yes. The standard process includes a review stage before scheduling. You can nominate one or more authorised reviewers and agree how quickly comments should be returned.

What happens if we miss the review deadline?

The post remains unpublished unless a different rule has been agreed. We can move it to a later slot rather than publish an unapproved version.

Do you respond to comments and messages?

Community management can be included with clear response guidance and escalation rules. Sensitive, contractual or unusual conversations should be passed to an appropriate person in your team.

Which platforms do you support?

The exact mix depends on your audience and objectives. Common programmes include LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook and short-form video platforms, but we avoid recommending channels without a clear reason.
