# Inovantage content approval procedure

Use this procedure for website insight posts and adapt the same controls for social media content.

## Roles

- **Author:** prepares the brief, source material, draft and corrections.
- **Reviewer:** checks facts, clarity, brand, privacy, confidentiality, claims, links and accessibility.
- **Publisher:** confirms that approval is recorded and releases the exact approved version.

One person may hold more than one role in a small business, but the workflow should still be completed deliberately. For a stronger control, use a second GitHub collaborator and require one pull-request approval before merging to `main`.

## Status definitions

1. **Draft** — incomplete and not approved for external use.
2. **In review** — complete enough to check; comments and corrections are expected.
3. **Ready** — reviewer has approved the exact copy and creative.
4. **Published** — approved version is live and the public URL is recorded.

## Required checks before approval

- The intended audience and purpose are clear.
- Names, dates, prices, claims and links are verified.
- No client, employee or personal information is disclosed without authority.
- No invented case study, testimonial, result, statistic, award or certification appears.
- AI-assisted text has been checked against the approved source material.
- The call to action is accurate and operational.
- Images are licensed or owned and include useful alternative text.
- The preview has been checked on desktop and mobile.
- The page title and search description are useful and not misleading.
- Legal, financial, medical or regulatory statements are reviewed by an appropriate person.
- The exact version being approved is identifiable.

## Website post workflow

1. Open `/admin/` and sign in with a GitHub account that has repository access.
2. Create or edit an Insight post.
3. Save it as a draft. Decap CMS creates a content branch and pull request.
4. Move it to **In review**.
5. Wait for Netlify to finish the Deploy Preview.
6. Open the preview and complete the required checks above.
7. Record corrections in the same draft; do not create competing copies in chat or email.
8. When approved, move it to **Ready**.
9. Publish through the agreed merge process.
10. Confirm the production page, sitemap and links after Netlify deploys `main`.

## Social media workflow

1. Approve the campaign or monthly content plan.
2. Create a complete brief and identify source material.
3. Prepare caption and creative as one reviewable item.
4. Run the internal quality check.
5. Place the full post in **In review** with a named reviewer and deadline.
6. Record approval of both copy and creative.
7. Schedule only the approved version.
8. Check the platform preview immediately before scheduling.
9. Monitor comments and messages after publication.
10. Record learning for the next cycle.

## Emergency changes

Urgent content still needs an accountable approver. Record who requested it, who checked it, what was changed, why the normal timetable was shortened, and who will monitor the result. Never skip privacy, confidentiality, accuracy or safety checks.
